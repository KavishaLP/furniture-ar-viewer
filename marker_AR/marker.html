Four quick steps

  1. Hold the picture in view. Point your camera at one of the four pictures you saved or printed. The matching furniture appears on it. Need the pictures? Download them here.
  2. Keep the one you like. Use the arrows if you want a different design, then tap Keep this piece.
  3. Move it around. Tap a piece, drag to slide it, pinch to resize, or tap Turn left / Turn right.
  4. Add more, then finish. Scan the next picture to add another piece. When the set looks right, tap Show my room to hide the scanner.
