How it works

About a minute, from opening the page to furniture on the floor.

  1. 1

    Allow the camera

    Your phone asks once. Tap Allow. The live picture is only shown on your screen — nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

  2. 2

    Pick a piece

    Choose a chair, sofa, cupboard or table, then swipe through the designs until one feels right.

  3. 3

    Place it, then nudge it

    Tap to put it down. Drag with one finger to move or turn it. Pinch with two fingers to make it bigger or smaller. Don’t like it? Remove it and try another.
