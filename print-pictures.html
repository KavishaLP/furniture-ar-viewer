Get the pictures first

The camera looks for these four pictures. Download them to your phone, then open them on another screen — or keep them handy — and open the camera.

The easy way

  1. 1

    Download the pictures

    Tap Download under the one you want, or tap Download all to save all four at once.

  2. 2

    Show one picture to the camera

    Open a downloaded picture on another phone or computer, or hold it on screen. Keep it flat, fully visible, and in good light.

  3. 3

    Open the camera

    When the pictures are ready, tap Pictures are ready — open camera. Point at one picture and the matching furniture appears.

Each picture opens a different piece of furniture. Download the one you want to try — or download all four to build a full set.
